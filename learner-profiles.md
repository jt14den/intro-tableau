## Priya, a policy analyst

Priya works for a city planning office and is comfortable in Excel and Google Sheets, sorting, filtering, and building pivot tables, but has never used Tableau or any dedicated visualization tool. Her team has been asked to present traffic-safety trends to a city council committee, and static spreadsheet charts aren't cutting it for that audience.

**Goals:** Learn enough Tableau to build a small set of clear charts and a one-page dashboard she can present without needing a developer's help.

**Potential barriers:** No background in data visualization design (chart type selection, color use), and some unfamiliarity with dragging fields between shelves rather than writing formulas, since that interaction model is new coming from spreadsheets.
